## 本地介绍及来源

### Colosseum, Roman Forum and Palatine Hill

Ancient Rome's largest amphitheatre, part of the archaeological park with the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill.

古罗马最大的圆形竞技场，与古罗马广场、帕拉蒂尼山同属考古公园。

[来源](https://colosseo.it/en/tickets/colosseum-roman-forum-palatine/)

### Borghese Gallery

An art museum in Villa Borghese.

位于博尔盖塞别墅的艺术博物馆。

[来源](https://galleriaborghese.beniculturali.it/en/wp-content/uploads/Guidelines-on-the-new-procedures-for-visits.pdf)

### Domus Aurea

Nero's archaeological palace complex.

尼禄宫殿的考古遗址。

[来源](https://colosseo.it/en/tickets/domus-aurea/)

### Capitoline Museums

Rome's civic museum collection on Capitoline Hill.

位于卡比托利欧山上的罗马市立博物馆馆藏。

[来源](https://www.museicapitolini.org/en/node/1011298)

### St. Peter's Basilica

The principal basilica of Vatican City.

梵蒂冈城的主要大教堂。

[来源](https://www.basilicasanpietro.va/en/faq/is-it-possible-to-book-entrance-to-st-peter-s-basilica)

### Baths of Caracalla

A large Roman imperial bath complex open as an archaeological site.

作为考古遗址开放的大型古罗马帝国浴场。

[来源](https://cultura.gov.it/luogo/terme-di-caracalla)

### Trevi Fountain

Rome's monumental Baroque fountain.

罗马的巴洛克式纪念性喷泉。

[来源](https://www.comune.roma.it/web/it/notizia/biglietto-dingresso-fontana-di-trevi.page)

### Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel

The Vatican art collections, including the Sistine Chapel.

梵蒂冈的艺术收藏，包括西斯廷礼拜堂。

[来源](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en.html)

### Pantheon

A preserved ancient Roman monument.

保存至今的古罗马建筑。

[来源](https://direzionemuseiroma.cultura.gov.it/en/pantheon/)

### Castel Sant'Angelo

A historic fortress and national museum beside the River Tiber.

位于台伯河畔的历史堡垒和国家博物馆。

[来源](https://direzionemuseiroma.cultura.gov.it/en/museo-nazionale-di-castel-santangelo/)
